# Source and novelty audit

**Frozen search date:** 28 September 2026.
**Boundary:** the supplied version-0.1 manuscript and referee report; primary
arXiv full texts and abstracts; relevant publisher records; targeted semantic
searches on arbitrary on-site fields, isotropic spin-1 nonintegrability,
Reshetikhin conditions, symmetry averaging and quasicentroids.

This is a targeted comparison, not a systematic review or a historical-priority
certificate. Some conventional keyword searches returned irrelevant pages;
those results were not used. Semantic Exa searches supplied relevant primary
research, and the load-bearing source was directly opened with web browsing.
No uninspected abstract has been treated as a fully audited proof.

## Load-bearing external theorem

**Hokkyo, A. (2025). Rigorous test for quantum integrability and nonintegrability,
version 3.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2501.18400v3

Inspected setup, equations (4) and (6), Theorems 1 and 2 and the surrounding
explanation. The setup permits site-dependent on-site terms. The first hypothesis
is injectivity at each interaction endpoint. The second identifies the global
strictly two-local leading solution space with the span of the interaction
Hamiltonian. The paper proves both from its local leading-rigidity definition,
proves full rank sufficient, and gives exact local pair certificates for the
additional two isotropic rank-deficient rays. The reduction excludes a three-site
quantity whose commutator is at most two-local, not merely an exactly conserved
three-site charge. Its bound is 3 <= k <= floor(N/2).

The complete external proof was not re-proved or formally verified in this
revision. The manuscript identifies the dependency explicitly.

## Primary comparators for the new field scope

**Hokkyo, A., Sanatani, M., & Chiba, Y. (2025). Absence of nontrivial local
conserved quantities in the spin-1 bilinear-biquadratic chain and its anisotropic
extensions. Physical Review Research, 7, 043297.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04945v4
https://doi.org/10.1103/vjtq-sz21

Inspected the model and theorem statements in the full text. This source treats
the bilinear–biquadratic chain with a uniform uniaxial anisotropy and generic
anisotropic models with additional internal symmetries. It is not represented
as only a zero-field or only an isotropic classification. The revised result's
distinct quantifier is over every site-dependent Hermitian local matrix.

**Park, H. K., & Lee, S. (2024). Proof of nonintegrability of the spin-1
bilinear-biquadratic chain model.**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23286
https://arxiv.org/html/2410.23286

Consulted the record and model/theorem material surfaced by the primary full
text. This independent field-free work is credited. Publication coordinates
not confirmed in this search are not invented; the manuscript cites the preprint.

**Shiraishi, N., & Yamaguchi, M. (2026). Dichotomy theorem separating complete
integrability and nonintegrability of isotropic spin chains. Physical Review B,
113, L241111.**
https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/lcn4-tbcm
https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14315v4

Inspected the publisher record and relevant full-text theorem/proof material.
This is the main zero-field arbitrary-spin comparator. Its use of symmetry to
constrain the leading and correction sectors is acknowledged as part of the
existing framework. The current paper's new averaging argument eliminates
arbitrary fields at the level of the almost-conservation equations and gives
an exact quotient formulation.

**Metadata discrepancy:** the publisher and arXiv HTML v4 give Mizuki Yamaguchi;
the arXiv abstract record encountered in the search gave Mizuki Sanatani. The
manuscript follows the publisher/full-text author list. The version-0.1 author
attribution has been corrected, not propagated from the abstract metadata.

**Sengoku, S., & Watanabe, H. (2026). Absence of nontrivial local conserved
quantities in a class of U(1)-symmetric spin-1 chains.**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17548

Abstract and model/theorem passages surfaced from the primary PDF were used for
scope comparison. The proof was not independently audited. This work treats
physically important rank-deficient internal-symmetry families; it is not
subsumed by a claim about generic unrestricted interactions.

## Reshetikhin comparisons

**Hokkyo, A. (2026). Integrability from a single conservation law in quantum
spin chains. Physical Review B, 114, 055108.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20713v5
https://doi.org/10.1103/y66t-t8zw

Inspected the current theorem formulation and publication metadata. It concerns
a specific boost-generated conservation law and its commuting hierarchy.
It is cited for positioning, not substituted for the revised 2025 reduction.

**Sanatani, M., Shiraishi, N., & Ishii, F. (2026). A simple necessary and
sufficient condition for Yang–Baxter integrability.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.29660v1

Inspected the full Reshetikhin identity and Theorem 1's formulation, including
the diagonalisability hypothesis and the regular difference-form R-matrix
conclusion. Its proof is not independently certified here. The result applies
to the full identity, not merely the partial trace O. Our field-eliminated
quotient vanishing is not equated to satisfying this full identity.

**Grabowski, M. P., & Mathieu, P. (1995). Integrability test for spin chains.**
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9412039
https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/28/17/013

Historical context for tests based on local conservation. No new proof step is
made dependent on an uninspected detail of this paper.

## Algebraic attribution

**Andruszkiewicz, R. R., Brzeziński, T., & Radziszewski, K. (2024).
Lie affgebras vis-à-vis Lie algebras.**
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.01996

Read the exact quasicentroid and centroid statements on printed page 8, including
a rendered screenshot of that page. It states that the quasicentroid equals the
centroid for centreless perfect Lie algebras, with attribution to Leger–Luks
Theorem 5.28. This supplies attribution for the scalarity mechanism. The present
manuscript retains its self-contained sl_d proof.

**Leger, G. F., & Luks, E. M. (2000). Generalized derivations of Lie algebras.
Journal of Algebra, 228, 165–203.**
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021869399982509

Bibliography and relevant theorem attribution checked through the preceding
primary paper. The original complete article was not inspected. This indirect
route is disclosed in the manuscript instead of implying direct proof inspection.
