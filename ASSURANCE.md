This is an unrefereed mathematical-physics candidate. Written universal arguments, exact finite computations, and public availability are separate evidence.

- Author claim: the theorems in paper.tex, with their field, symmetry, leading-rigidity and range assumptions.
- Producer checks: eight finite suites; two separate integer implementations; exact rational and modular certificates; portable dual/consistency witnesses and tamper rejection.

- Formal verification: not performed.
- Independent reproduction/reimplementation: not established by coordinated producer checks.
- Human specialist or editorial peer review: not established. The supplied review is retained privately by input hash; reviewer identity and independence are not authenticated.
- Novelty: bounded comparison to primary predecessors; not a priority guarantee.
- Applied impact: no experiment, thermalisation, chaos, ETH, or performance advantage is established.

The exact quotient decides existence of a support-reduced three-site commutator under leading rigidity. Its vanishing is not general integrability.
