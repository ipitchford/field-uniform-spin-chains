## Load-bearing reduction

Hokkyo, *Rigorous test for quantum integrability and nonintegrability*, arXiv:2501.18400v3, https://arxiv.org/html/2501.18400v3. Rechecked setup, conditions (4),(6), and Theorems 1 and 2. On-site terms may differ at every site. Boundary injectivity and the strict two-site leading space condition are required. The manuscript proves them using local leading rigidity, full-rank quasicentroid argument, and two deficient-case certificates. Range-three almost-conservation means commutator support at most two, not exact conservation. The all-range implication is imported, not formally re-proved by the finite checker.

## Closest conceptual comparator

Shiraishi and Yamaguchi, *Dichotomy theorem separating complete integrability and nonintegrability of isotropic spin chains*, https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14315v4; https://doi.org/10.1103/lcn4-tbcm. Rechecked zero-field Hamiltonian (1), dichotomy statement and correction-sector proof following (18). SU(2) and time reversal already constrain the correction. The current candidate's additional step is averaging the affine equations with arbitrary symmetry-breaking field variables, yielding the field-uniform quantifier. It does not claim the established zero-field integrable lines as discoveries.

## Author metadata reconciliation

Publisher record and https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04945v4 show Yamaguchi; https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04945v4 shows Sanatani. Follow publisher and HTML full text; do not speculate about the difference.
